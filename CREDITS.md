# Credits

## The color data

Sanzo Wada, *Haishoku Soukan* (配色総鑑), 1933–34. The combinations.

Seigensha Art Publishing, *A Dictionary of Color Combinations* (配色事典), 2010, ISBN 978-4-86152-247-5. The edition the colors were read from. The book, its cover, and its prose remain with the publisher.

Bill Mageni ([@Bjoseph23](https://github.com/Bjoseph23)) compiled and maintains this dataset. The compilation is [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-DATA). The code is [MIT](LICENSE).

Matt DesLauriers, [dictionary-of-colour-combinations](https://github.com/mattdesl/dictionary-of-colour-combinations). An independent check that the same colors sit in the same combinations. That project’s own license covers his files, not these.

## Studio photographs

The outfit, room, and facade photographs were generated for this repository. They are original assets of the project, released under the same [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-DATA) terms as the data compilation. No stock photo, Sketchfab model, or Quaternius file is included.

Masks that mark cloth and room surfaces were derived from those photographs in `scripts/build_studio_assets.py`.

## What we did not ship

- Google’s `<model-viewer>` (Apache-2.0) and the Khronos Materials Variants shoe (Shopify, CC BY 4.0) are real retailer-style references. They are not in this repo.
- Poly Haven assets are CC0. None are bundled here.
- Quaternius assets may be used in a product, and the license forbids redistributing the files themselves. They are not in this repo.
- MakeHuman’s bundled meshes are CC0 and its code is AGPL-3.0. Neither is bundled here.
